Screenshots of the real app, still in development.

Satin Player is a web music player for people who keep their own library of audio files. You import a folder, the library is saved on your device, and everything plays without an account and without a connection.

Your music is yours: the app can only talk to its own domain and to the accounts server, so your files never leave the device.

At a glance

  • 10equalizer bands, ±12 dB
  • 7equalizer presets
  • 5,000tracks in performance tests
  • 2ReplayGain modes: track or album

Behind the project

What it does
  • Your library Import files or a whole folder. Tags, covers and the quality of each file are read in the browser, and the library is saved in IndexedDB.
  • Sound FLAC and other formats, ReplayGain, a ten-band equalizer, crossfade, shuffle, repeat, synced lyrics and a sleep timer.
  • Queue and playlists Drag to reorder, multi-select with batch editing, a history of what already played, and saving the queue as a playlist.
  • Look The accent comes from the cover that is playing. Four backgrounds: Cover, Rhythm, Still and AMOLED.
  • Optional account It only syncs your playlists. Without an account you still have everything else.
Technical Development

Built with React 19, TypeScript and Vite. The library lives in IndexedDB and is rescanned by differences, with the metadata read in a worker so the interface never waits for it.

The content security policy is closed: fonts and assets are self-hosted and the app can only connect to its own domain and to the accounts server. Animations only touch transform and opacity.

Queue and playlists stay virtualized even while you drag: the lifted row follows the pointer outside React and only its transform changes each frame.

Unit tests with Vitest cover the audio, queue and library logic, and browser scripts walk the real flows: lists, drag and drop, and mobile gestures.

Design

The expanded player puts the cover across the full width and lets its color flood the background. The interface has no fixed brand color; the logo, in sand gold on warm black, avoids the category's clichés: no sound waves, headphones or musical notes.

Project Status

The web app is running on a preview deployment while I refine the experience and prepare it for a public launch. Android and iPhone versions are planned, and the core features that run on your device will remain free and ad-free.
