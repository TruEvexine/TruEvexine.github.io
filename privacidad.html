What is saved

Only your language choice (English or Spanish), in your own browser. It never leaves your device and you can erase it by clearing the site data.

Quote calculator

The numbers are calculated on your device. Nothing you pick is sent to me or stored: it only becomes a message if you decide to open it in WhatsApp.

Email and WhatsApp

If you write to me, the message goes through your email provider or WhatsApp and reaches me with whatever you decide to include. Their own privacy policies apply.

Gameplay video

The SIN GUARDAR video loads from YouTube only when you press play, using its privacy-enhanced mode. Until then, YouTube receives nothing from you.

Hosting

Like any host, Cloudflare processes technical data of each visit, such as the IP address, to deliver and protect the site. I don't use that data to track you.

Questions

Want to know more, or ask me to delete a message you sent me? Write to me.
